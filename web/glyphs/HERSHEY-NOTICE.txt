web/glyphs/hershey-*.json are converted from the Hershey fonts (.jhf) distributed at
https://github.com/kamalmostafa/hershey-fonts (source: http://emergent.unpythonic.net/software/hershey).

Acknowledgements required by the distribution's use restriction:
- The Hershey Fonts were originally created by Dr. A. V. Hershey while working at the
  U. S. National Bureau of Standards.
- The format of the Font data in this distribution was originally created by
  James Hurt, Cognition, Inc., 900 Technology Park Drive, Billerica, MA 01821.

Regenerate with: node tools/make-hershey-glyphs.cjs <directory containing futural/rowmans/scripts/cursive .jhf>
